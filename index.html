Profitability Decline Investigation

Problem

Profit fell for three quarters while each department reports satisfactory results. Integrate Marketing, HR, Operations and Finance data to expose cross-functional causes.

Algorithm flow

InputProcessDecisionOutput
Pull quarterly data: Marketing, HR, Operations, Finance
Build profit tree: Profit = Revenue − COGS − Marketing − Payroll − Logistics − Other
Variance analysis: Δ of each line vs. previous quarter
Cross-functional links: leads↑ but conversion↓; overtime↑ → quality↓ → returns↑; delays → refunds
Does a department KPI 'pass' while its downstream cost rises?
YES → flag hidden interaction & quantify $ impact
LLM writes root-cause narrative per quarter
Profit bridge (waterfall) + cross-department findings

Pseudocode

tree = profit_tree(finance)
for line in tree:  line.delta = line.q[t] - line.q[t-1]
for rule in CROSS_RULES:       # e.g. (overtime up, defects up, returns up)
    if rule.upstream_up(t) and rule.downstream_up(t):
        findings.append((rule, rule.impact(finance, t)))
bridge = sorted(tree + findings, key=abs_delta, reverse=True)
narrative = LLM("Explain the profit change using", bridge)
